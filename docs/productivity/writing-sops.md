## What it does

Turns a repeatable staff task into a practical standard operating procedure. It chooses detail by what the reader needs to complete the task, handle likely exceptions, and recognize success.

It reads available materials before asking questions and keeps uncertain details visible to the requester. The procedure describes the current workflow; proposed improvements stay separate.

## When to reach for it

Type `/writing-sops`, or the agent reaches for it automatically when a task fits.

- Create a guide for a recurring office process or software task.
- Capture a handoff that currently depends on one person's memory.
- Update instructions after a workflow changes.

## Practical task guides

A useful SOP explains where to start, which job or record to choose, what to do, and how to tell the work is finished. Troubleshooting follows symptoms the reader can recognize. Screenshots and checklists earn their place by making the task easier.

For JGI work, the skill loads a separate reference with local terminology and examples. It verifies current workflow details before turning those examples into instructions.

## Common questions

**At JGI, is an SOP the same as a WP?**

No. JGI uses Work Procedures for calibration procedures required for its A2LA accreditation. SOPs cover other procedures, including software operation. Calibration instructions belong with the applicable WP sources and requirements.

**Do these SOPs need audit paperwork?**

JGI's SOPs are for end users and will not be audited. The skill adds formal approvals, document numbers, or revision tables only where an applicable process or the requester needs them.

**Can it write a guide before someone tries the steps?**

Yes. It delivers a draft and identifies the checks still needed. It does not claim a staff walkthrough happened, or fill gaps with invented instructions.

## It's working if

- The intended employee can find the starting point and follow the steps without unwritten explanations.
- The guide explains relevant exceptions and what proves completion.
- Software labels and screenshots match the workflow staff actually use.
- Unanswered questions are visible to the requester rather than hidden as guesses in the instructions.
