# JGI context for SOPs

## Scope confirmed by Jeff, October 24, 2026

At Johnson Gage and Inspection, Inc., **Work Procedures**, or **WPs**, are specifically calibration procedures required for JGI's A2LA accreditation. **SOPs** document other procedures, including software operation. These SOPs are for staff usefulness and will not be audited.

Write ordinary JGI SOPs as practical task guides. Choose structure and detail for the end user. Default to a short purpose, actionable steps, relevant exceptions, and a clear finish. Formal document control and approval paperwork are not default requirements.

If a request includes calibration instructions, identify that portion as WP work and use the applicable calibration sources and JGI requirements. An SOP can explain how to find or use an existing WP, but should reference that WP rather than invent or replace its technical instructions. Keep mixed operational and calibration content clearly separated.

## Keep guidance current

Confirm the installed workflow before documenting a changing application. Earlier discussions included planned shipping changes and SOP updates tied to those changes; a prototype or accepted proposal alone does not establish released behavior.

Distinguish completing a task from receiving a reassuring message. Use the actual evidence relevant to the workflow, such as a saved label, a matching attachment, or the intended record showing the change.

Specific contacts, escalation routes, current application labels, and recovery permissions must come from current evidence or the requester. Prior chat context supplies useful questions, not permission to change a business record.
